# VD-vision-pass-rulings-90zdiw: four north-star rulings, taken on delegated authority

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-11
**Context:** The vision estate was written in one day, before any code existed. One package has since been built — `kernel/internal/store` — at a cost of nine adversarial review rounds. A four-lens vision pass tested the estate against that evidence and returned four questions that touch the ratified north star (VD-north-star-6io56h), which I do not change unilaterally. The founder reviewed the questions and delegated the calls with the words "go with your recommendations, if you still agree" — an instruction to re-examine, not merely to execute. I re-examined all four and changed one before applying it.
**Decision:**
1. **The dissolution ladder gets a FLOOR, and the headline stands.** "Testing 100% redundant as a human activity" is about AUTHORSHIP; detector power is about whether anything is still checked. The week proved they are orthogonal — an agent wrote 6,687 test lines for 2,165 production lines (L1 by default) and five assertions could not fail. An invariant with no measured kill rate is not on the ladder at all. Recorded as a strengthening, not a hedge: the estate promised to remove humans from testing, and the real prize is removing unmeasured belief.
2. **The residual delta is ADDED as a second review surface; the consequence brief is NOT replaced.** The per-change loop demonstrably did not converge (rounds 6-9 each found a fault in the previous round's fix), but stopping a bad change at the boundary is the standalone enterprise pitch, and residual COMPLETENESS is unsolved — today it is five hand-adjudicated items on one package. Adding is cheap; replacing bets a working mechanism on an unbuilt one.
3. **Regeneration-as-repair is added as build-order item 2b, gated on measured lock power.** Patching is the measured expensive act (1-3 new defects per cycle, four consecutive cycles) while generation was cheap. But the strongest counter-evidence in the pass is ours: a 1,609-line lock with 70 invariant rows still shipped five residuals, any of which a regenerated implementation could satisfy-and-reopen. So 2 and 2b are one mechanism at two confidence levels, and 2b unlocks only where aggregate detector power is high.
4. **Verification is scheduled on BELIEF ACCUMULATED — adopted, but SHARPENED before adoption.** The recommendation as written was untestable, which is precisely the defect the pass indicts, so it does not go in without a measurable trigger: belief accumulates as *fix cycles since the last independent verdict* and as *invariants changed without a re-measured kill rate*, both countable from the ledger. I also corrected the stated cost — the headline economics are about HUMAN attention, and calibration is machine work, so belief-interval interruption does not put a human back in the loop.
**Consequences:** Two of the estate's most quotable sentences now carry qualifications (the ladder floor, and "we sell certainty" retired in favour of the enumerated residual). That is surface a keynote has to handle, and it is the correct trade: both qualifications are things the first package measured, and shipping them unqualified means the differentiator is disproved by its own first instance. Ruling 4 adds a scheduling constraint whose proxy may not survive contact with data — it is falsifiable by design and comes back out if the two counts do not predict defect injection across three packages. Ruling 3 makes detector power load-bearing for the code-as-cattle wedge, which raises the stakes on ADD-2 considerably: without measured power, 2b never unlocks.
**Revisit when:** the fix-induced regression series exists (needs the `review.verdict` kind added to P1) and covers three packages — that single dataset can falsify rulings 2, 3 and 4 at once; or per-invariant power measurement proves expensive enough that ruling 1's floor becomes an unmeetable promise, in which case it re-cuts as a sampling claim rather than an absolute.
