# VD-build-laws-c76aki: the build process runs under VERA's own laws (dogfood scaffolding)

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Context:** Process tooling fails in a recognizable pattern: maintenance cost nobody measures, enforcement that stops firing without anyone noticing, warnings that never become errors, and duplicated sources of truth that drift. Those failures take hold when the build process is exempt from the principles the product preaches. VERA's credibility depends on its own build being the first demonstration.
**Decision:** The Build Laws in CLAUDE.md are binding: (1) generated artifacts are `@generated`-marked, hook-locked, freshness-checked; (2) one home per datum — the CLAUDE.md table is normative; (3) every hook has a self-test in `make hooks-test`, which is part of `make check`, and blocking hooks fail CLOSED when a dependency is missing; (4) every advisory check is registered in docs/gates.md with owner + ISO expiry — graduate or delete, and a dateless or expired row fails `make check`; (5) meta-tax measured by `make meta-tax` against the budget in gates.md; (6) spec-before-code for kernel packages; (7) lessons compile to enforcement — a `LESSON:` seen twice becomes a check or is explicitly retired; (8) no new dependency without a decision record.
**Consequences:** Slightly more ceremony at bootstrap (founding records, the gates table) in exchange for mechanically-checked honesty about our own process. The scaffolding is deliberately tiny — a handful of hooks, one self-test, one Makefile, five commands — and anything more must displace something.
**Revisit when:** meta-tax breaches budget two measurements running (simplify), or the kernel's own gate engine can absorb a Makefile check (migrate it, delete the script).

**Amended 2026-08-11:** a NINTH law was APPENDED — *no actor grades its own work*. It has the most evidence behind it of any law here: nine adversarial rounds on the first package, nine non-empty verdicts, and the author still shipped a test that could not fail in the same session he was fixing tests that could not fail.

It was first INSERTED at position 8, which moved "small kernel, boring tech" to 9 and silently invalidated nine existing "Law 8" citations — every one still resolving to a real law, every one naming the wrong one. Reverted the same day. **Law numbers are now permanent identifiers: new laws append, none is inserted, re-ordered or re-used, and a retired law leaves a numbered tombstone** (CLAUDE.md, and `scripts/law-citation-lint.sh` detects a renumber mechanically). Invariant numbers already worked this way; the laws did not.
