# VD-p7-snapshot-provider-skip-2026-10-01: retain the conditional snapshot-provider skip

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-01
**Context:** P6 closed with the conditional snapshot provider intentionally skipped. P7 Gate 1
required a founder decision before any provider specification, feed selection, or implementation.
No concrete lawful export feed or immediate product requirement was designated.

**Decision:** Retain the P6 snapshot-provider skip. No snapshot provider, vendor, export feed, width
exception, schema, event kind, or implementation is authorized by this decision. The pinned,
source-neutral provider contract remains preserved for a later, separately authorized phase.
P7 planning may continue only within existing feeds and separately authorized slices.

**Consequences:** The core Proofbound goal—connecting business intent to exact code, independent
verification, deployment, and observed outcomes—continues through the existing repository-native
and accepted feeds. No external snapshot source is assumed, invented, scraped, or treated as
lawfully available. A future provider requires a new decision naming one concrete lawful export
feed and explicitly authorizing the narrow boundary expansion.

**Revisit when:** A concrete external source, a specific product need for its historical
point-in-time records, and its permitted export boundary are identified together.
