# VD-verification-asymmetry-2dyjnd: finding defects converges; fixing them does not — mechanism is the only lever that has moved either

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Context:** Two days on the git connector (2026-08-12/13) produced the cleanest dataset this project has on where correctness actually comes from, extending the nine-round store evidence behind VD-fix-discipline-0e0tnz. The numbers: round 1 found 15 real defects in code that was `make check` GREEN with an author-run mutation sweep; the remediation closed 9 of 15, and five of the six failures were one shape — the reproduction was fixed while the invariant stayed false. Round 2 then found 21 findings against that remediation, with the adjudicator's summary verbatim: "Both SPECs are less true than they were before round 2's remediation on at least one axis each." The author was measurably MORE careful in the second pass and it changed nothing — fix rate and defect-introduction rate stayed roughly equal. Meanwhile every intervention that was a MECHANISM converged immediately: six gates were built across the two days, every one caught a live defect on its first real run against the repo, every one had a bug its own self-test caught before it was trusted, and lesson classes answered with a blocking script have recurred zero times against 3–5 recurrences for prose-answered classes. Three findings sharpen the asymmetry beyond "review more": (1) a REVIEWER'S verified finding came with an unverified remedy that was wrong — it would have refused a real decision id — so even expert review earns trust only about what is broken, never about what to do; (2) tests that were real, non-vacuous and mutation-killing still could not discriminate the property they were named for, because their fixture held the relevant variable constant; (3) the author broke the append-only numbering rule while enforcing it elsewhere the same day, and the identical rule with a detector (`law-citation-lint`) has never drifted — knowing a rule is not the same as being unable to break it.
**Decision:** The asymmetry is adopted as a design premise for VERA itself, not just as build hygiene, because this repo is the testbed for what the product must assume about agent-built software:
1. **A warranty claim is never grounded in author self-assessment, greenness, or care** — all three were empirically worthless as evidence here. It is grounded in mechanical witnesses that fail closed, each with calibration controls spanning independent dimensions (a compile-time control and a test-time control cannot substitute for each other — two controls that resolve through the same mechanism pass and fail together).
2. **Findings and remedies carry separate trust.** A verified finding transfers zero verification to its proposed fix; the product's data model and the build process both treat "what is broken" and "what to do" as independently evidenced claims.
3. **Claims are stated as harms/invariants, not reproductions.** A check keyed to a reproduction certifies the example; the five-of-six closure failures are what that costs.
4. **Every "cannot / never / always" is a test or it is a defect.** Two such comments were false in one package in one week, and both were load-bearing premises.
**Consequences:** Build cost shifts permanently toward gates and harnesses — measured meta-tax for the two days was 12% of countable commits, inside the 25% bootstrap budget, and it bought six mechanisms that each paid for themselves within hours. Adversarial review remains necessary (it found what every gate missed) but its output is treated as a stream of claims to re-verify, not verdicts to obey. For the product roadmap this record is the empirical grounding for Task 5+ design: witnesses must bind to CONTENT (the `allowed-survivors` allowlist keyed to `line#ordinal` rotted the moment the file was edited — evidence keyed to position expires silently, evidence keyed to content_sha expires loudly), and verdicts must be durable artifacts (see VD-verdicts-are-artifacts-rl0rab).
**Revisit when:** a remediation cycle completes with zero new defects found by the following adversarial round (the asymmetry may be closing); or a gate with passing self-tests ships a false-negative that a careful human WOULD have caught (the premise "mechanism beats care" has a counterexample worth studying); or meta-tax breaches budget two measurements running (the premise is being over-applied).
