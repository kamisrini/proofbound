  and emits **AUTHORIZE / DENY**, failing closed:

  - RL-9 (counterexample lethality evidence present and reproduced on the pre-repair impl, §5.5),

  - RL-11 (one `L@r+1` canonical digest used throughout),
  - RL-4 / RL-5 (the D-a representativeness record and D-b non-author isolation attestation present at
    their §3.1 homes),

  - RL-8 (the declared Law-4 limitations recorded).
- The `docs/verification/regeneration/` record homes (§3.1) and a `docs/gates.md` advisory row

- **No regenerator / step-3 re-derivation and no step-4 candidate verification** — those are the full
  loop, out of this slice (re-derivation is manual; §5.1 deferred).
- **No non-zero `U_BUDGET`** (RL-12 is future-only, no self-test here — CL-2).
- No change to O-1 (detector-power) semantics, the behavior-lock contract, the mutation harness, or
  any package SPEC beyond what the shell gate needs; no new provider, event kind, or wire identity.

## Acceptance boundary

self-test proving each RL control load-bearing; `make check` green; and an independent non-author
package verdict of the exact implementation revision (Law 9). The owner remains the final acceptor.
Producing the gate does not by itself authorize the regenerator (step 3) or the full loop.
