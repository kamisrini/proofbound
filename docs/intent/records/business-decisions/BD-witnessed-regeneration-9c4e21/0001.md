# Witnessed regeneration (vision build-order #2b)

**PROPOSED — awaiting owner acceptance.** This repository-native business decision proposes that
Proofbound build the witnessed-regeneration capability described in
[vision-2028.md](../../../../vision-2028.md) §9 item 2b and the completion plan
[product-completion-plan-2026-11-05.md](../../../plans/product-completion-plan-2026-11-05.md) §A:
regeneration-as-repair (discard a failing implementation and re-derive it against the lock + the new
counterexample), gated so it unlocks only where aggregate **detector power** is measured and high.

The first committed step is the **detector-power metric + regeneration-trust gate** whose contract is
the owner-accepted-pending design spec
[P7-2b-detector-power-spec-draft1.md](../../../plans/P7-2b-detector-power-spec-draft1.md) (three-round
non-author review, ACCEPTABLE). The regeneration loop itself is a later step under its own spec.

The owner remains the acceptor (Law 9). Until this record's status flips to `accepted`, no governed
work derives authority from it, and the requirement and change intent it authorizes stay `proposed`.
