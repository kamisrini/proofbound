# Self-hosted build is governed: the acceptance gate is a warranted witness

**ACCEPTED by the owner 2026-11-09** (recorded by the agent as scribe; the owner is the acceptor,
Law 9). The owner chose this path (recommendation (a)) to give governed maintenance a real home in
the intent fabric instead of landing it HEAD-only and reconciling after the fact (the
`governed-without-intent` debt reconciled 2026-11-06,
[2026-11-06-governed-without-intent-reconciliation.md](../../../verification/governance/2026-11-06-governed-without-intent-reconciliation.md)).

This repository-native business decision records the owner's standing authorization that Proofbound's
own build — as its first tenant (self-hosting; `vision-100x.md`, the 2029 "Proofbound builds
Proofbound" milestone) — is governed under its own warranty. The acceptance gate (`make check`) is a
warranted witness and must be **deterministic and reproducible**; a flaky, order-dependent, or
environment-dependent gate run is a defect. It authorizes the requirement [[BR-gate-integrity-4b2e08]]
(`proposed` — it goes `active` on its non-author requirement review) and, through it, intent-bound
governed fixes to gate integrity such as [[CI-flake-ephemeral-port-7d5f10]] (`accepted`). The owner
remains the acceptor of the implementation that follows (Law 9: a non-author verdict still gates the
code).
