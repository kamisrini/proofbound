# Proofbound's own build is sequenced into governed releases

**ACCEPTED by the owner 2026-11-10** (recorded by the agent as scribe; the owner is the acceptor,
Law 9). The owner chose this path — a new governance intent chain — to give release/roadmap decisions
a real home in the intent fabric rather than land them HEAD-only and reconcile after the fact (the
precedent decision commit `daf9fe4`, which regenerated the same governed index without a trailer,
in fact shipped a red gate; this chain prevents repeating that).

This repository-native business decision records the owner's standing authorization that Proofbound's
own build — as its first tenant (self-hosting; `vision-100x.md`, the 2029 "Proofbound builds
Proofbound" milestone) — is delivered as a sequence of **shippable releases** laid over the phase
record, and that the decisions drawing and moving that line are **governed**. The first release line
is recorded in [`VD-release-line-kwdh4v`](../../../decisions/VD-release-line-kwdh4v.md) with the
maintained scope split in [`RELEASE-PLAN.md`](../../../plans/RELEASE-PLAN.md). Because recording such a
decision regenerates the governed `@generated` `docs/decisions/INDEX.md`, it is governed work and must
be intent-bound. It authorizes the requirement [[BR-release-line-governance-7ff96f]] (`proposed` — it
goes `active` on its non-author requirement review) and, through it, the governed change intent
[[CI-release-line-4069b0]] (`accepted`). The owner remains the acceptor of any implementation that
follows (Law 9).
