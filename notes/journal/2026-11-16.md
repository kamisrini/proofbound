## Post-commit finding: the kernel has never bound a governed commit's Intent trailer

After pushing `a028984`, `proofbound intent check --commit <sha>` printed `intent=none` — and so did
the precedent governed commit `34af46d`. Root cause, from `kernel/internal/connector/git/SPEC.md`
("Only a final Git trailer block may populate `IntentTrailers`") and `git interpret-trailers
--parse`: in **17 of the 30** governed commits (including the precedent and today's) the `Intent:`
line sits in its own paragraph with a blank line before the `Co-Authored-By` paragraph, so git's
final trailer block contains only `Co-Authored-By`; the `Intent:` line is *body*, and per INV-28
("subject/body mentions never create an `intent_refs` entry") the kernel records **no claim**. The
other **13** place it correctly inside the final block — the right convention exists here and
drifted; it was never enforced. The shell gate `scripts/intent-delivery-applicability.sh`
greps `%B` for `^Intent:` anywhere, so `make check` is green. Split brain: the gate says every
governed commit is intent-bound; the Trust Record says 17 of them are not. Not a regression of
today's commit — it matches the convention exactly — but it means the record is weaker than the
gate claims, which is the one thing this project exists to prevent.

LESSON: class=harness-checks-wrong-thing response=notes/state.md (owner decision) → hook case
The harness (shell gate) verified the *presence* of a line, not the *binding* the kernel performs
— Law 9's corollary in the wild: a verdict is only as good as its harness. Fix forward, under the
existing gate-integrity home (`BD-self-hosted-gate-integrity-9a7c31` → `BR-gate-integrity-4b2e08`
O-1): (1) change the commit convention — `Intent:` goes in the final trailer block, adjacent to
`Co-Authored-By`, no blank line; (2) make `intent-delivery-applicability.sh` parse the final trailer
block (`git interpret-trailers --parse`) instead of grepping `%B`, with a self-test that proves a
body-only `Intent:` line FAILS; (3) add a kernel-side bind check to the gate (`intent check
--commit HEAD` must print a bound intent for an applicable commit). The 17 already-pushed body-only
commits cannot be re-trailered without rewriting pushed history (not proposed); they are recorded
as a known tombstone in the deferral register pending the owner's call.
