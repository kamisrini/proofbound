### LESSON: pipe-swallows-exit on a validator

`bash scripts/p6-artifact-integrity.sh --check | tail -5; echo exit=$?` reported `exit=0` while the
script had actually exited 1 ("audit artifact stale" — my new verdict changed the verdict count and
the `@generated` audit artifact `docs/verification/p6-artifact-integrity.md` needed `--write`). The
`$?` was `tail`'s, not the script's. This is the exact failure CLAUDE.md warns about for `make check`
("never pipe through anything in a `&&`/pipe chain — a red gate reads as green"). Generalize: when
checking a gate's pass/fail, never read `$?` through a pipe — capture the command's own status.

Then the bigger finding: a web-fetch agent confirmed the official v16.4 source folder ships
`postgresql-16.4.tar.bz2` with `.md5` and `.sha256` checksum files **only — no detached `.asc`
signature, no `KEYS` file** in the source tree. So the B1 decision's recorded premise ("postgresql.org
tarballs are signed by a published PGDG key") is FALSE for source tarballs; PostgreSQL historically
published PGP-signed *checksums* via the announce list, not a detached signature in the folder.
Surfaced this honestly to the owner as a fork rather than silently downgrading the provenance model.
Owner chose the **least-expensive, light-touch mechanism**: pin the published SHA-256 and verify a
pristine-upstream tarball fetched over HTTPS from the official domain. Honest guarantee recorded as an
owner-accepted residual: integrity + official-domain TLS trust, not an offline signer-identity
signature (checksum and tarball share an origin). Corrected the VD (dated amendment), the brief
(DECISION banner), and the slice plan.

Executed the verify (downloads outside the clone, `--max-redirs 0`, requested URL == effective URL,
HTTP 200): measured SHA-256 `971766d6…386d6f` == published `.sha256`, size 24765786 == the folder
listing (md5 also matched, informational). Committed the source manifest
(`docs/verification/p7-replay-lock-fixtures/postgres-source-16.4.json`) and the evidence record
(`.../p7-replay-lock-prerequisites/postgres-source-16.4-verify-20261101.md`). Unit 1's provenance
check passes under the adopted mechanism; a reproducible verifier script + self-test and a non-author
verdict (Law 9) still remain before unit 1 counts as accepted.
