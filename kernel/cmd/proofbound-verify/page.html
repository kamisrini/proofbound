Proofbound {{VERSION}}. This page runs entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded, and it works offline.

The vendor's signing key fingerprint, received by another channel: an email, a contract, a call. Never copy it from the bundle you are checking: a bundle cannot vouch for itself.

What the answer means

SIGNATURE ONLY The file was signed by the key you typed, nothing in it was changed, and it obeys the warranty rule: by its own account, every promise of the change was reviewed by someone other than its author and met. Only a check against the repository confirms that account.

INVALID Something above failed. The reason says what.

What this page cannot check: that the commit and its reviews really exist in the source repository. For that, run proofbound warranty verify <file> --repo <clone> --trust-key <key>, or proofbound ui --bundle <folder> --repo <clone>, which can answer VALID.
